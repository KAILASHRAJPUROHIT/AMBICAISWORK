Display-only status rules implemented
- CLEAR / GREEN:
  invoice_amount == bank_amount, bank_amount > 0, confidence == HIGH.
- PARTIAL PAYMENT / BLUE:
  0 < bank_amount < invoice_amount.
- PENDING BANK / YELLOW:
  bank_amount == 0.
- RISK / MISMATCH / RED:
  difference < 0, mismatch status, risk status, error status, or ambiguous match.
- REALIZING CHEQUE / BLUE:
  payment mode includes CHEQUE and stored status is not already cleared/verified/paid.
- ADVANCE PENDING / PURPLE:
  advance payment exists and invoice is not fully settled.

Status explanations added
- CLEAR:
  Invoice amount fully matched by verified payment evidence.
- PARTIAL PAYMENT:
  Partial payment received. Outstanding balance remains.
- PENDING BANK:
  No verified bank payment received yet.
- RISK / MISMATCH:
  Payment evidence conflicts with invoice value.
- REALIZING CHEQUE:
  Cheque deposited. Awaiting bank clearance.
- ADVANCE PENDING:
  Advance received. Final settlement pending.
