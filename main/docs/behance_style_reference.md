# Jewellery Campaign Style Reference

Synthesized from 11 Behance galleries (Zaveri Jewels, BONEUR PARIS, Regal Emeralds:
Polki Symphony, Tytler Couture 2022, Smudge Art, Jos Alukkas Campaign, Bhima
Jewellers Calendar '26, Loukya, Wedding Guests by Kushal's Jewellers, Keerthy
Suresh x Jos Alukkas, JOSCO Jewellers '25). Read this before drafting new Aradhya
concept batches — pull specific combinations from here rather than re-inventing
generic "garden"/"studio" ideas.

## Genres observed (pick one deliberately per concept, don't blend randomly)

1. **Maximalist editorial/fantasy** (Zaveri Jewels) — painterly/dreamlike graded
   skies, jewel-toned flowers (orchids, marigolds) framing or obscuring the face,
   uncut-diamond/polki suites, tousled hair with flowers tucked in, bold red lip.
2. **Dainty minimalist studio** (BONEUR PARIS) — flat warm-grey seamless
   backdrop, single hard directional side light (or moody low-key with lens-flare
   prism artifacts), thin stacking rings/bangles/chains, intimate hand-to-face
   crops, natural film grain.
3. **Warm bronze studio regal** (Regal Emeralds: Polki Symphony) — mottled/
   textured warm brown-bronze backdrop (NOT flat — has visible painterly texture
   and a soft radial vignette), center-parted sleek low bun, gaze often turned
   away/downward rather than direct eye contact, hand touching the necklace,
   muted rosy-brown eyeshadow + matte lip. **Closest existing match to our own
   brand studio look — mine this one hardest.**
4. **Outdoor bridal couture** (Tytler Couture) — real trees/outdoor natural
   light, flower garlands (mogra/jasmine) in hair, dramatic fabric movement/
   swirl, full bridal jewellery suite worn together (maang tikka + matha patti +
   choker + haar + bangles + rings + nath), petals scattered on ground.
5. **Moody fine-art digital painting** (Smudge Art) — not photography but a
   useful MOOD reference: night, dark water with ripples, single hero zone
   (feet + anklet) lit dramatically against near-black surroundings.
6. **Festive candid campaign** (Jos Alukkas, Kushal's) — genuine laughing/
   smiling expressions (not just serious/regal), group-of-friends dynamic,
   pastel floral or solid pastel outfits (blush, sage-green, cream) UNDER heavy
   gold jewellery (contrast, not matching tone-on-tone), palm-leaf/garden venue,
   golden-hour backlight through foliage, marigold garlands/petals as props.
7. **Heritage interior** (Jos Alukkas "Kapil Ganesh" shot) — saturated color-
   blocked wall (deep red), antique wooden furniture, brass/vintage props
   (lamps, perfume bottles, boxes), stained-glass window casting colored light
   patches, seated-at-a-desk pose, temple jewellery + roses in hair.
8. **Temple ruins / dynamic heroic** (Loukya) — ancient carved stone pillars
   (Hampi-style), stormy or bright sky, LOW CAMERA ANGLE looking up at the
   subject, wind-caught pallu/dupatta mid-motion, atmospheric dust haze, bold
   saturated saree colors with contrasting multicolor borders (navy+emerald+
   gold+red; vermillion+gold pinstripe).
9. **Golden-hour lotus pond** (Bhima) — misty backlit haze over a lily/lotus
   pond, reclining/lounging pose (not standing), pastel ice-blue embroidered
   organza as a saree color choice, antique gold coin (kasu mala) necklace.
10. **Marigold flower market** (Bhima) — baskets/heaps of orange marigolds as
    background fill, deep warm wall behind, puffed-sleeve contrast blouse (red)
    against olive-gold saree, nath + maang tikka + vanki armband, festive/
    Diwali mood.
11. **Palace jharokha + diya lamps** (Keerthy Suresh x Jos Alukkas) — carved
    latticed windows with visible light shafts streaming through the pattern,
    rows of lit brass diya lamp stands, a shallow pool with floating lotuses and
    diyas, chin-resting-on-hand contemplative pose, oxblood/maroon silk.
    **Single best lighting/mood reference of the whole set — atmospheric depth
    via visible light shafts + practical lamp light, not just soft key light.**
12. **Luxury lifestyle/yacht** (Keerthy Suresh x Jos Alukkas) — contemporary
    Western silhouette (sequined draped gown, not saree), teal channel-tufted
    boat seating, open sea horizon, designer handbag prop, relaxed reclining
    arm-along-seatback pose. Use only if a modern/non-traditional angle is
    ever wanted — off-brand for Aradhana's current traditional positioning.
13. **Maximalist floral greenhouse throne** (JOSCO) — a striped vintage armchair
    fully enveloped in an explosion of mixed real flowers (roses, hydrangea,
    larkspur — not a blurred garden bokeh, an immersive flower wall), backlit
    golden haze through glass conservatory windows, Western ballgown
    silhouettes (off-shoulder, corset-bodice).

## Poses & camera angles worth adding to our rotation

- **Hand touching/framing the necklace** (draws eye to hero piece) — used in
  genre 3, 7, 11.
- **Chin resting on hand, elbow on knee, seated** — contemplative, unhurried
  mood (genre 11).
- **Low camera angle looking up** at a standing subject gripping a pillar/
  architectural element, fabric caught mid-wind (genre 8) — we have not used
  this angle at all yet; all our concepts so far are eye-level or above.
- **Reclining/lounging full-body**, one leg bent, body draped diagonally across
  a ledge/bench (genre 9, 12) — we have only done seated or standing so far.
- **Both hands framing the face from below** (dainty rings/bracelet hero,
  genre 2) — intimate, works well for ring+bracelet combined zones.
- **3/4 turn, gaze cast away from camera** rather than direct eye contact
  (genre 3, 8) — softer, more editorial than our current mostly-direct-gaze
  default.
- **Genuine laughing/smiling candid**, not composed serious expression —
  genre 6. Worth adding at least 1-2 concepts per batch with real joy rather
  than uniformly serene/regal.

## Color combinations worth trying (outfit + jewellery + backdrop)

- Navy blue saree + emerald-green blouse + gold/red/green contrast border
  (Loukya) — bold, unexpected, works against sandstone.
- Vermillion red + gold pinstripe, no contrast blouse — simpler, high-impact.
- Ice-blue/powder-blue embroidered organza against antique gold jewellery
  (Bhima) — pastel-cool saree against warm-gold jewellery, a contrast we
  haven't tried (we've mostly paired warm-on-warm).
- Olive-gold saree + deep pink/magenta blouse (Jos Alukkas interior shot).
- Mustard-gold satin/silk, worn alone without a contrast blouse, radial warm
  vignette backdrop (Regal Emeralds) — our closest existing template.
- Sage/olive-green pastel group outfits under gold-and-white kundan jewellery
  (Kushal's mehndi shot).
- Deep red wall + dark green Kanjivaram saree + magenta blouse (heritage
  interior shot) — jewel-tone-on-jewel-tone against a saturated wall, not a
  neutral backdrop.
- Blush/cream + rose-pink pairing (Kushal's golden-hour shot) — softer, two
  guests in complementary but not matching tones.

## Lighting techniques

- **Mottled/textured painted backdrop with soft radial vignette** (warm
  bronze-brown) rather than a flat seamless — reads as more premium/editorial
  than flat grey. Worth replicating in our `location` prompt language: "a
  richly textured, mottled warm bronze backdrop with a soft vignette, not a
  flat seamless."
- **Visible light shafts through a carved/latticed pattern** (jharokha
  windows, stained glass) — much more atmospheric than generic "soft window
  light"; specify the light itself should be visible as shafts/patterns, not
  just its effect on skin.
- **Backlit golden haze/mist** (lotus pond, greenhouse) — a hazy atmosphere
  the light rakes through, not clean/crisp daylight.
- **Practical lamp light as a scene element** (diyas, string lights) — lets
  warm light sources exist IN frame, not just off-camera.
- **Low-key moody with lens-flare/prism artifacts** (BONEUR PARIS) — a
  deliberately imperfect, "shot through glass/crystal" look for a more
  intimate, less polished mood.

## Props worth introducing

- Flowers as **compositional framing device**, not just background: orchids/
  baby's breath partially obscuring the face or held near-camera in soft focus
  (Zaveri).
- Brass diya lamps (lit, in-frame) — festive/temple mood.
- Vintage furniture + antique brass/glass decor objects (heritage interior).
- Marigold baskets/heaps as a full background fill (market stall mood), vs.
  marigold garlands hanging overhead (mehndi/ceremony mood) — two distinct
  uses of the same flower.
- A shallow reflecting pool with floating flowers/diyas.

## How to use this file

When drafting a new `CONCEPTS` batch (see `aradhya_realism.py`'s
`build_prompt()`), pick ONE genre above per concept rather than mixing
several, and pull the specific color/prop/pose/lighting language verbatim
where it fits the jewellery hero zone being shot. Genres 3 and 11 are closest
to Aradhana's existing brand look and are the safest default; genres 8, 9, 12,
13 introduce genuinely new poses/angles we haven't used and are good picks
when the goal is deliberately expanding variety.
