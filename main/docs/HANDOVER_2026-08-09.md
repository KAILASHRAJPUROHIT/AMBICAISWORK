## 2. RESOLUTION — what Klein actually sees (the biggest lever)

Source plates are 6192x4128 = **25.6 MP**. Both live workflows shrink hard
before the VAE encode.

| workflow | node | scaling | Klein receives |
|---|---|---|---|
| stage 1 isolate | `comfy_local_img.py:220-222` | `ImageScaleToTotalPixels`, **`nearest-exact`, 1 MP** | ~1 MP |
| stage 2 edit (diptych) | `comfy_local_img.py:260-262` | `ImageScale` to `TILE x TILE`, `lanczos`, **`crop: center`** | **512x512 = 0.26 MP** |

`TILE = 512` (`comfy_local_img.py:53`). That is a **98.6% reduction**, and
`crop: center` also *crops* to square — anything off-centre is discarded.

**Measured on the equivalent Variant C path** (same model, same idea), with
only reference resolution changed:

```
1.0 MP nearest-exact ->  34.9s   TP22_10 tassel drawn with 3 chains  (truth 2)
2.0 MP lanczos       ->  57.8s   3 chains
4.0 MP lanczos       -> 135.1s   2 chains  CORRECT
8.0 MP lanczos       -> 396.4s   2 chains  no further gain, 2.9x cost
```

Ring spokes over the same change: ~10 thick bars -> ~15 fine wires.

**So "Klein cannot count fine detail" was only half true.** Small repeated
structure was being aliased away by our own resampler before generation.

**Two changes, in the file that runs:**
1. `comfy_local_img.py:221` `nearest-exact` -> `lanczos`. Strictly better,
   identical cost. `lanczos` is already used at line 261, two lines away.
2. `TILE = 512` -> 768 or 1024. Raises what Klein sees in the live diptych.
   Latent becomes 1536x768 or 2048x1024, so it costs generation time —
   measure before adopting.

Neither was applied: a batch was running.

## 3. PRODUCTION BUGS FIXED (live, keep)

### 3.1 `category_topology` — every paired category resolved to SINGLE
**The most valuable fix of the day.** `topology_for()` matched
`normalise_category(...)` against exact sets, but real folder names carry
stock numbers — `"56 TOPS 22"`, `"6 BALI 22"` — and every one normalised to
`'generic'`, falling through to SINGLE.

Consequence: the output gate expected 1 piece, a correct pair rendered 2, and
the item was rejected as `PIECE_COUNT_MISMATCH`. Tops, earrings, jhumka, bali
and bangles — 407 items. Very likely the reported "10+ errors in 55-65".

Fixed with `_resolve()`: strip stock numbers, then word-match **longest key
first** so `ring` can never shadow `earring`. 18/18 folders correct.

> `'earrings'` contains `'ring'`, and `'earrings'.endswith('rings')` is True.
> This bit three times in one session, twice in throwaway analysis code.
> Never write an ad-hoc category filter; call `category_topology._resolve`.

Used at `app.py:2183` to write `.count_confirmation.json` for every pair.

### 3.2 Crash guards in `variant_c_pipeline`
- `crop_info['occupancy']` formatted while `None` -> `TypeError`
- `expected_components` referenced before assignment -> `NameError`

### 3.3 `local_reader.free_reader()` frees ALL ollama models
Two generations were refused — `KLEIN_RESOURCE_GUARD: VRAM free 3030 MB <
5000 MB` — with nothing of ours loaded. Cause: `qwen2.5:7b`, a **text** model
loaded by another tool on the machine, holding 4.4 GB. Freeing only our own
model left it. On an 8 GB card the reader must clear the **card**.

Check with `GET http://127.0.0.1:11434/api/ps` before blaming ComfyUI.

### 3.4 `free_klein()` does not check the queue — hazard, unfixed
`health.fix()` checks `queue_running` before freeing; `free_klein()` does not.
Calling it directly can interrupt a live generation. It was called mid-run
today; the jobs happened to survive. **Guard it.**

## 6. WHAT WAS LEARNED ABOUT PROMPTS (transfers to any path)

1. **A false clause beats the reference image.** LR22_10: a correct 22% crop
   was overridden by "filigree scrollwork", "granulation", "white enamel" —
   Klein drew a different ring.
2. **Vagueness is not safe either.** Removing unverifiable technique words
   produced the *worst* output of the day — an abstract band with an invented
   diamond, despite "introduce no stone tint" in the prompt.
   **=> Records must be SPECIFIC AND TRUE. Neither "say less" nor "say more".**
3. **Closed enums force invention.** A model asked to pick a decorative
   technique picks one. Adding *"answer 'none' if it is plain undecorated
   metal"* fixed it immediately — same mechanism as *"plain gold with no
   stones is very common, answer false"*.
4. **The word "reflection" was suppressing specular highlights** in the
   Variant C prompt (`"No cast shadow or reflection"`), making metal read as
   CGI. Check `jewellery_image_policy` for the same phrasing.

## 8. LOCALISATION — solved, and where

Finding the ornament on a cluttered showroom plate defeated six methods:

```
colour_gold ............ kept 87-92% of the plate
sam_locate ............. returned the PRICE TAG
focus/Laplacian alone .. fragments, then 50% boxes
RMBG-2.0 ............... whole frame (documented 2026-08-02)
qwen2.5-VL grounding ... clipped the jhumka bells, kept the barcode
VLM self-check loop .... answered "not clipped" on a severed bell
```

What works (`local_reader.tighten_box`): take a rough box, **expand
generously**, then inside that window keep pixels that are **gold AND in
focus**, and pick the blob whose centroid is **nearest** the rough box.

- gold alone locks onto background bokeh (gold, but blurred)
- sharpness alone locks onto velvet weave and the printed label
- only the subject is both, because the camera focused on it
- "must overlap the box" discards a clipped bell; nearest-centroid does not

Also: **`crop_guard` cannot detect a loose crop.** It scored a
100%-of-plate frame at 1.000. Occupancy must be checked separately —
`design_scan.CROP_MAX_OCCUPANCY = 0.35`.

## 12. LATE SESSION: SHORT KLEIN PROMPTS ARE THE MAJOR FIDELITY LEVER

### Official guidance checked

Black Forest Labs documentation:

- https://help.bfl.ai/articles/7592221790-how-do-i-generate-quickly-with-flux-2-klein
- https://docs.bfl.ai/guides/prompting_guide_flux2
- https://docs.bfl.ai/flux_2/flux2_image_editing

Relevant rules:

- put the critical instruction first;
- 30-80 words is normally ideal;
- describe the required result positively;
- avoid negative-prompt-style lists (`do not`, `never`);
- assign each reference image one clear role;
- Klein has no prompt upsampling and is step-distilled for 4 steps.

The production ladies-ring prompt was about 250 words. It repeated many
negative constraints and contained a direct contradiction: `one continuous
finger band/shank` versus `open, overlapping and unjoined ends`. It also mixed
design preservation, category education, placement, reconstruction and failure
prevention in one instruction.

### LR18_13 controlled comparison

Clean isolated source:

`reports/adaptive_klein_2026-08-09/ring_roi/LR18_13_gpt_isolated_white.png`

Long-prompt output:

`output_comfy_local/comfy_local_adaptive_ring_LR18_13_gpt_isolated.jpg`

Failure: Klein converted the open bypass ring into a conventional closed,
stacked band. It removed one endcap and changed the other into a gemstone.

Short prompt used (56 words):

```text
Place the exact gold bypass ring from the left reference upright on the ring box in the right scene. Preserve its photographed shape and orientation: two overlapping open ends, two contrasting metal endcaps, twin central rails, and both fish-scale lattice sections. Keep the jewellery design unchanged. Match only the destination lighting, scale, contact shadow and background.
```

Short-prompt output:

`output_comfy_local/comfy_local_adaptive_ring_LR18_13_short_prompt.png`

Result: major improvement. Open bypass topology, overlap, central rails and
both lattice sections survived. Remaining errors: silver endcap became dark;
dark endcap became gold; orientation and small lattice geometry shifted.

This controlled test used the same isolated source, background, deterministic
ring seed, 768 tile and 4 steps. Prompt was the meaningful changed variable.

### LR22_436 generalisation test

stone and simplified some radial divisions.

### Production conclusion

Prompt overload was a confirmed major failure source. Adopt this template:

```text
Place the exact [ornament] from the left reference [placement] in the right scene.
Preserve its photographed [orientation/design]: [1-3 visually certain critical
features]. Keep the jewellery design unchanged. Match only the destination
lighting, scale, contact shadow and background.
```

Rules:

1. Keep prompts between 30 and 80 words.
2. Include only 1-3 image-specific, visually certain facts.
3. Put topology/silhouette first when structurally unusual.
4. Use positive target descriptions instead of prohibition lists.
5. Never call a reflective region a stone unless facets and a setting are clear.
6. Do not inject the old generic category lecture.
