PAYMENT PROOF ATTRIBUTION FIX REPORT

Scope:
- Fixed payment proof attribution/display only.
- Reconciliation amount matching was not changed.
- Payment status logic was not changed.
- No financial records were modified.

Root cause:
- Reconciliation payment breakdown used:
  payment.utr_reference or bill.reference_no
- For split invoices such as SS-518, the CASH payment had no payment-level UTR.
- The CASH row inherited bill.reference_no 524249677405 and displayed the SMS proof for the separate UPI payment of 600.

Fix:
- Real payment rows now use only payment.utr_reference for proof lookup.
- CASH/manual rows do not inherit bill.reference_no.
- SMS/Bank proof attaches only when:
  proof.utr_reference == payment.utr_reference
  proof.amount == payment.amount
  payment mode supports digital proof.
- CASH/manual rows return:
  source = "No digital proof / manual cash entry"
  proof_status = "NO_DIGITAL_PROOF"
  proof_url = null
- Same-UTR but different amount returns:
  proof_status = "MISMATCH"
  proof_url = null
  proof_label = null
- Frontend carries proof_status and displays:
  "No digital proof / manual cash entry"
  or
  "Proof amount mismatch"
  instead of a misleading clickable proof.
