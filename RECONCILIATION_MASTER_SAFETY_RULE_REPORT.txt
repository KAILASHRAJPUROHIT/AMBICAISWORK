- This rule overrides exact amount match, zero difference, HIGH confidence, and CLEAR display status.
- The rule is read-only display logic only.

- confidence: Medium
- status: ACCOUNTANT APPROVAL REQUIRED
- status_color: PURPLE
- review_required: true in the response/display only
- status_explanation: Old gold exchange or advance payment requires accountant approval even though amounts may match.

Normal exact-match behavior preserved
- Normal UPI/CASH/CARD/BANK exact matches can still display confidence HIGH and status CLEAR.

- Overpaid or risk/mismatch rows still display RISK / MISMATCH with RED status color.
